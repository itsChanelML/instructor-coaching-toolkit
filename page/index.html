The rubric

Eight dimensions, scored independently — never averaged into one number. A single "Not yet" on accuracy or accessibility is disqualifying regardless of how strong everything else is.
Not yet
Developing
Solid — certification bar
Exemplary
01

Technical accuracy

Correct, current, and honest about the edges of what's known — "I don't know, let me check" said without losing authority.

02

Pacing & time management

Protects load-bearing sections' time budget even when something upstream ran long, and notices mid-session when the room is behind.

03

Clarity of explanation

Builds from what the room already knows, in the room's vocabulary — not the instructor's internal mental model.

04

Checking for understanding

Real checks — a wrong-answer-shaped question, a poll — not "does that make sense?", which almost nobody answers honestly.

05

Hands-on lab facilitation

Walks the room rather than waiting for raised hands; tells "stuck on a typo" apart from "stuck on the concept" quickly.

06

Handling questions

Answers the question asked. Redirects a rabbit-hole without making the asker feel shut down.

07

Engagement & energy

Genuine presence, not performed enthusiasm — and responds to a flagging room instead of pushing through the plan.

08

Inclusivity & accessibility

Never leaves people silently behind on assumed context, and never makes a struggling learner an example.

Feedback: Situation → Behavior → Impact → Suggestion

Every piece of feedback uses this shape — praise included. "Great job today" is applause, not feedback; it doesn't say what to repeat.
Strength, written so it's repeatable
SITUATION
Step 2, right after the first person hit the "loop never terminates" bug.
BEHAVIOR
Asked "what does len(messages) do at the top of your loop?" instead of debugging it themselves.
IMPACT
The participant fixed it in under a minute, and three nearby people used the same diagnostic on their own code, unprompted.
SUGGESTION
Ask that question to the whole room proactively at the start of Step 2 next time — it prevents the bug as often as it fixes it.
Gap, written as one fixable thing
SITUATION
Step 4, introducing RETRY vs. ESCALATE.
BEHAVIOR
Showed both branches of code back-to-back before asking which applies to a hallucinated tool name vs. a renamed column.
IMPACT
About a third of the room could recite both branches but couldn't say, unprompted, which failure mode each is for.
SUGGESTION
Ask "whose fault is this — the model's or the data's?" before showing either branch. Land the concept, then show code as the answer.

Train-the-trainer pipeline

Nobody advances on a calendar date — only on the rubric.
01

Shadow

Observes a certified instructor teach the full session, using the rubric themselves — first exposure to the material live, and low-stakes practice with its vocabulary.

ExitCan describe a specific moment in SBI shape, unprompted.
02

Co-teach

Delivers one or two modules solo while the certified instructor runs the rest and floats during the candidate's modules.

ExitSolid on clarity, checking understanding, and engagement for their modules.
03

Solo teach, observed

Runs the entire session. The observer stays silent — no floating, no rescuing — and scores the full rubric.

ExitSolid or better on all eight dimensions, across at least two sessions.
04

Ongoing calibration

Quarterly re-observation, plus coaches independently scoring the same recorded session before comparing notes — keeps "Solid" meaning the same thing as more coaches join.

ExitN/A — recurring, not a one-time gate.

Worked example — Stage 3 observation

A constructed example: candidate Jordan Lee, delivering engineer-agent-adoption-workshop solo for the first time, scored against the actual rubric above.
Decision: not yet certified. Six of eight dimensions at Solid or better — but Pacing and Lab Facilitation share one root cause: three participants hit Step 2's known "loop looks stuck" bug within two minutes of each other, and each was diagnosed from scratch at their desk instead of addressed to the room once. That cost 8 of Step 2's 20-minute budget and meant two other affected participants were never seen. One more Stage 3 session, focused only on that, is the entire next step — the other six dimensions don't need re-proving.
